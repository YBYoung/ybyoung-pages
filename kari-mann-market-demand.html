Is local demand for dental care falling in Cape Coral?

How much of the drop in Kari's Google profile calls is the local market, and how much is her profile's share of it. Google profile calls are the yardstick throughout.

The short answer

Partly the market, probably not most of it, and we cannot measure the split with confidence. In July and August 2026, both Google sources we have show fewer dental searches in her area than a year earlier. Her calls fell further than either one. Before July, the search data and her calls do not move together at all.

  • July and August, year over year: her profile calls fell 32% (264 to 180). Google Trends interest in "dentist" in the Fort Myers-Naples area fell 11%. Google's keyword volumes for dentist searches in Cape Coral fell 24%.
  • The local drop is bigger than the state's. Over the same two months, keyword volumes fell 6% across Florida and rose 10% across the US. Trends fell 9% across Florida, close to the local figure.
  • April to June does not line up. In April 2026 her calls were up 14% on April 2025 while Cape Coral keyword volume was down 64%. In May her calls fell 30%; Trends rose 48% and keyword volume fell 52%. The sources disagree with each other and with her.
  • Nothing shows the local dental business shrinking. Lee County dental offices had 2,353 jobs in March 2026, up 7.1% on a year earlier, against 0.9% for Florida. Cape Coral's population grew 1.35% to July 2025. Both stop before her May drop.
  • Our other practices do not share her drop. Of 35 practices, 18 had fewer summer calls and 17 had more; the typical change was about 2% down. Kari's was 25% down. They are in other markets, so this speaks to what is typical, not to Cape Coral.

How sure this is. Low to moderate. Both search sources show sudden jumps in the same month in every area at once, from Cape Coral to the whole US, which points to changes in how Google measures rather than in what people search. Neither Google nor the trade press has documented either jump, so we cannot correct for them. The July and August comparison is the cleanest stretch we have, and it is two months.

Implant searches

We cannot measure implant demand in her area. Keyword volume shows implant searches down 72% in Cape Coral for June to August, but it also shows them down 45% across the whole US, while Google Trends shows US interest in "dental implants" rising over the same months. One of the two is wrong at the national level, so neither local implant figure can be trusted. Before July 2025, Trends for implants in the Fort Myers-Naples area read zero in 7 of 19 months and never above 20 in the rest, which means the local sample was too small for a steady reading.

What would settle it

  • Google Ads data, if the practice runs Google Ads. An account's impressions divided by its impression share estimates how many eligible searches happened in its target area, month by month, from Google's own count rather than a rounded estimate. That separates demand from share directly.
  • Kari's own points of contact for more months. Her log covers June to September 2026 only. A year of it would show whether her total fell or only the profile's part of it.
  • The next dental jobs release. April to June 2026 for Lee County should be published around December 2026, and would be the first to cover her May drop.
